# Module portrait generation

Built-in image-generation tool, one request per portrait. Same selected PNGs installed in demo and live at `orchestrator/src/atlas_orchestrator/web/greek/aeolus.png` and `orchestrator/src/atlas_orchestrator/web/greek/demeter.png`.

## Aeolus final prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: standalone mythological portrait icon for a dark navy dashboard, square image.
Style/medium: dramatic painterly fantasy portrait with realistic sculptural face, luminous metallic details and cinematic chiaroscuro, like a premium classical deity medallion.
Composition/framing: one centered head-and-shoulders deity, face large and readable at 48px, three-quarter view, contained within a complete circular luminous thin rim filling roughly 90% of the square. Dark navy outside the circle, no rectangular border. Restrained atmospheric detail behind the face.
Constraints: exactly one standalone icon, no letters, no text, no numerals, no watermark, no additional panels, no lower badge or typography. Ensure strong facial legibility and a complete circular rim.
Subject: Aeolus, Greek keeper of winds, dignified mature male deity with flowing silver hair and short silver beard, calm powerful expression, classical draped shoulders. Curling wisps of wind and soft clouds frame his silhouette.
Lighting/mood: icy cyan and silver face highlights, cool luminous cyan circular rim, dark blue shadows. Palette: icy cyan, silver, deep navy.
```
