# Asset provenance

## September 25 visual update

The Sol image `atlas-house-hilltop.png` is the owner's existing generated hilltop-house illustration, included with explicit owner approval. It depicts the owner's property and is not anonymous or fictional architecture. The separate Home hero remains unchanged.

`model-y.png`, `pickup.png`, and `motorcycle.png` were created with the built-in image-generation tool as photographic-style vehicle artwork. They depict a blue Tesla Model Y, a red Chevrolet Silverado, and a black Harley-Davidson Heritage Softail. They are not photographs documenting the owner's vehicles, and they do not verify a trim, equipment, or condition. Vehicle records remain fictional demo data.

`dallas-radar-demo.png` is built-in generated artwork showing a fictional storm over Dallas-Fort Worth. Both the image and demo card explicitly say it is simulated, not live weather. It contains no real warning or observation. The local application retains its separate configured radar behavior; this replacement is applied by the disconnected demo build.

Full generation prompts and asset filenames are recorded in `docs/visual-prompts-2026-09-25.md`.

The public home hero was produced with the built-in image-generation tool for this distribution. Its house, grounds, and architectural details are fictional. It contains no address or vehicle. The public avatar and favicon are code-authored SVGs, not portraits of real people.

The module tiles use the same owner-selected mythological character portraits as the private interface. Vesta, Sol, Titan, Oracle, Vulcan, Athena, and Olympus reuse its existing artwork. Aeolus and Demeter were created with the built-in image-generation tool to extend that set. These portraits are character artwork, not household photographs. The previous SVG monograms remain as unused compatibility assets.
