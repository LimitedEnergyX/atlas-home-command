# Asset provenance

## November 2026 public screenshots

Current interface screenshots live under `docs/images/live/`. They were captured from a sanitized live Atlas session for the November 2026 Home, Library, Energy, Command, and related module refresh. House imagery in those captures follows the owner's instruction to leave household house images as they are. Do not replace repo house artwork with unpublished private originals beyond what this distribution already includes.

`orchestrator/src/atlas_orchestrator/web/atlas-house-hilltop.png` remains the owner-approved Sol/energy house illustration shipped with this package.

`model-y.png`, `pickup.png`, and `motorcycle.png` were created with the built-in image-generation tool as photographic-style vehicle artwork. They depict a blue Tesla Model Y, a red Chevrolet Silverado, and a black Harley-Davidson Heritage Softail. They are not photographs documenting the owner's vehicles, and they do not verify a trim, equipment, or condition. Vehicle records remain fictional demo data.

`dallas-radar-demo.png` is built-in generated artwork showing a fictional storm over Dallas-Fort Worth. Both the image and demo card explicitly say it is simulated, not live weather. It contains no real warning or observation. The local application retains its separate configured radar behavior; this replacement is applied by the disconnected demo build.

The public home hero artwork used by the static demo was produced with the built-in image-generation tool for this distribution where applicable. Public avatar and favicon assets are code-authored SVGs, not portraits of real people.

The module tiles use the same owner-selected mythological character portraits as the private interface. Vesta, Sol, Titan, Oracle, Vulcan, Athena, and Olympus reuse its existing artwork. Aeolus and Demeter were created with the built-in image-generation tool to extend that set. These portraits are character artwork, not household photographs. The previous SVG monograms remain as unused compatibility assets.

Portrait generation notes for Aeolus and Demeter are recorded in `docs/portrait-prompts.md`.

The interface uses local SVG navigation symbols. The OpenAI mark identifies a provider and does not imply endorsement or grant trademark rights. Named products retain their respective trademarks. AI-generated artwork may have different copyright treatment across jurisdictions; MIT does not create rights that do not otherwise exist.
